Spell Link and Fogged Vision review, 2026-10-06

I reviewed all 127 Spell Link records and all 127 Fogged Vision records. All 127 abilities belong to the intended heroes. All 127 portraits depict the intended heroes. No record needs a different hero as its answer. The final catalog includes current hero names, corrected mechanics, and clearer clues.

The applied changes affect the text or names of 50 records. They include 37 Spell Link description corrections and 13 other records with name or distractor corrections. All 254 records now link to direct Valve hero or ability data. The changes preserve answer positions, question IDs, or image URLs.

The files are:

- `identity-question-patches.json` contains the applied ID-keyed changes, reasons, and source evidence, including the four final direct-ability source corrections.
- `identity-ledger.json` has one entry for every record, including the final content hash, applied status, source values, and ownership, name, option, image, and source checks.
- `identity-source-comparison.json` and `identity-differences.json` preserve the initial comparison. Their findings describe the original input. The four missing hero-API entries were later verified through the direct ability API.
- The repository file `src/data/spell-link-overrides.json` preserves the 37 reviewed descriptions for patch 7.41f. The sync helper applies them after selection, including when upstream supplies nonempty unreviewed text. The Muerta fallback in `src/data/ability-text-overrides.json` is also corrected.
- `identity-proposal-checks.json` records the checks on all proposed records.
- The repository file `tests/identity-facts.test.mjs` contains independent owner and localized-name mappings, plus seven regression checks. `identity-expected-mapping.json` also preserves those mappings.
- Four contact sheets and source manifests retain the visual and source audit evidence. Raw source images and full question input copies are omitted.
- `identity-final-checks.json` records the final source and content-hash checks. `test-results.txt` records the final focused test run.

Six clues present a level-one value as if it applies at every level. In addition to Sacred Arrow and Splinter Blast, these are Rolling Boulder, Healing Ward, Take Aim, and Fury Swipes. The final text preserves their identifying mechanics and states that the relevant effect depends on ability level. The independent values are Rolling Boulder base damage 60/70/80/90, Healing Ward duration 18/20/22/24 seconds, Take Aim passive range 160/240/320/400, and Fury Swipes expiry 8/12/16/20 seconds. [Earth Spirit data](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=107), [Juggernaut data](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=8), [Sniper data](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=35), [Ursa data](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=70).

Name removal causes a separate set of defects. Anti-Mage's clue changes the anti-magic shell into a "caster-magic shell". Night Stalker's clue replaces the nighttime condition with "at the caster". Astral Spirit, Time Dilation, Frost Shield, Overclocking, and Spirits contain broken noun phrases. The changes use complete descriptions that survive the existing hint builder. They also remove the direct hero aliases Barathrum and Lanaya from the Planar Pocket and Meld clues. Related ability names, such as Echo Stomp or Psi Blades, remain valid mechanical clues.

Current Valve localized names are Outworld Destroyer and Ringmaster. The original catalog used Outworld Devourer in both identity games and Ring Master in parts of Spell Link. These were label defects. The applied changes update every affected answer choice, reveal, and image alt text within these two question sets. The root review also standardized the hero picker and other games on these canonical names. [Outworld Destroyer data](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=76), [Ringmaster data](https://www.dota2.com/datafeed/herodata?language=english&hero_id=131).
