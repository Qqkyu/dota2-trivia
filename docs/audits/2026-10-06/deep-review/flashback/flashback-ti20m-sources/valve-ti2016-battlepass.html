Quests
Prior to each game, choose one of three Paths to determine your current objective. Complete the assignment and win the match to earn Battle Points and move to the next Quest. Earn enough points to raise your Battle Level and unlock new rewards. You can change your selection during the picking phase to match the needs of each game.

NEW THREE-STAR TIER SYSTEM
Every node of each Path has three tiers of difficulty, each offering a different Battle Point reward. Fulfill any tier to complete the Quest, and aim for the Three-Star objective to earn the maximum number of Battle Points for that node.
NEW THREE-STAR ALTERNATE STYLES
The final Quest of each Path will unlock a related Completion Reward unique to that Path, each of which can also receive a style upgrade for players who complete all Three-Star objectives along that Path.

NEW UNLOCKABLE QUEST PATH
Players that reach level 225 will not only receive a special Legion Commander Prestige item, they’ll also unlock a Quest Path designed specifically for her. Those who complete Tresdin's Path will receive a visually-unique enhancement for her Prestige item.
Wagering
Each week you will receive a balance of Tokens to use in the wagering system. Place a wager and back it up with a victory to earn additional Battle Points. Unspent tokens will not roll over to the next week's balance, so lay your limit on the line.

NEW HIGH ROLLER BONUS
Players can now receive an additional 500 tokens per week when their Battle Pass hits level 119, another 500 at level 235, and 1000 at level 395. Plus, all players that receive any of these upgrades will also be able to bet up to 500 tokens in a single game.
POSTGAME TIPPING
After destroying the enemy Ancient, reward a resourceful teammate or crafty opponent with some of your winnings. With more triumphs to come, your coffers are sure to be brimming.

Challenge

To kindle interest, you'll also start off with three free fireballs.

NEW
Weekend
Battle Cup
As we kick off The International season, the best professional players in the world prepare to carve their legacies into the summer tournament brackets. Soon the same challenges and thrills of competitive Dota will be available to everyone, with the launch of the Weekend Battle Cup.

MULTIPLE SKILL TIERS ACROSS FOUR DIVISIONS
During scheduled, weekly events, participants can party up and battle through eight-team, single-elimination brackets. Teams are placed in a skill tier against evenly-matched opponents competing in one of four geographic divisions.
The Spoils of War
Apart from the satisfaction of hard-fought victory, the Battle Cup prize pool includes Battle Points, a tournament trophy for your profile, and a special in-match item to display your triumphs to future teammates and enemies alike.
Battle Up
All Battle Pass owners will receive one free entry ticket, with additional tickets being available in the Dota 2 store. Receive five more Battle Cup Tickets at level 137. The Weekend Battle Cup season will run for the duration of the current Battle Pass, and the first weekend tournament begins in June. Stay tuned for more details.
Arcana Vote
Take part in Dota democracy and vote for which hero you think should receive the next Arcana set in this 103-hero runoff. Every Battle Pass owner will start with sixteen votes that are replenished each round. Use all your votes on one hero, or split them across multiple heroes. Once the final votes are tallied, the winning hero will be announced at The International 2016.

The river runs red...or green or purple! Starting at Battle Level 85, you'll receive mystical River Vials that allow your hero to change the substance and color of the river for everyone in the game. A vial can be equipped by claiming it from the in-game shop and cannot be applied to an already-modified river. As you level up, you'll gain access to new types of Vials as well as recharging your stock of previous Vials. Each vial comes with ten charges that last ten minutes each. The ultimate Vial, which turns the river into Blood, is available at level 2500 and comes with 50 charges.
