# Forced-color and muted-control fixes

The selected control checks found four display defects. Muting faded the volume text and slider. The party checkmark lacked contrast in the light forced-color palette. Shop tabs and calibration filters did not show which choice was selected. The [earlier screenshot review](../forced-colors-review/README.md) remains unchanged. These findings caused the intentional cancellation of [run 38046647944](../runs/38046647944/README.md) before its app build.

The product change affects three CSS modules. Muting now keeps the slider and value at full opacity. Forced-color party choices use a system-color checkmark with an empty accessible alternative. Shop tabs use an underline for the selected state. Calibration filters use different border styles for selected and unselected states. Keyboard focus remains separate from selection. Audio playback and stored sound settings did not change. The [six-file diff](changes-from-6e1eace.diff.txt) also adds the painted-control checker and the dark forced-color CI mode. [Before](before/source-hashes.json), [after source files](after/), and [source binding](source-binding.json) retain the exact tested bytes.

The test suite grew from 102 baseline assertions to 117 final assertions as it added normal-color contrast and mute-state coverage. These are assertions across controls and modes, not counts of distinct controls or defects.

The final Chromium run used version 149.0.7827.55 on the local development server. It completed at 11:10:27 UTC on October 10, 2026. It checked the home, party and calibration controls plus a shop fixture in desktop, light forced-color and dark forced-color modes. The report contains 12 page states and 3 fixtures. General keyboard journeys were disabled for this focused run. The control checks did exercise keyboard focus and party selection with Space. The command and checker snapshots are in [green](green/).

Before the fix, normal muted text had maximum painted contrast of 2.155 and the slider had 2.387. Their opacity was 0.42. After the fix, these values were 6.525 and 7.612 with opacity 1. Forced-color muted text reached 21 in both palettes. The final checks compare screenshot pixels against each crop's dominant background. They require enough pixels with contrast of at least 4.5 for the text and 3 for the slider and checkmark. They compare selected and unselected shapes without hover or focus. These crop measurements support the named controls; they do not certify page-wide contrast or physical displays. The [CSS color adjustment specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-adjust-1/#forced-colors-properties) explains forced-color property overrides and system colors.

The checker also needed corrections.
