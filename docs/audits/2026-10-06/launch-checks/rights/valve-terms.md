# Valve asset terms

Checked on 2026-10-06. This review records published terms and the app's current behavior. It does not claim a separate license for this app.

The Steam Subscriber Agreement, section 2.D, permits Valve game content in noncommercial fan art. It requires the creator to obtain rights to third-party content. Its named examples are fan art, fiction and audiovisual works. It does not name browser trivia applications. We therefore record a possible basis for the free fan project, with that scope limit. The agreement retains other ownership and use restrictions in sections 2.F and 2.G. [Steam Subscriber Agreement](https://store.steampowered.com/subscriber_agreement/#2)

Valve's video policy applies to videos. It permits certain video hosting and platform monetization, but expressly rejects distributing game assets separately. The app sends individual image and audio resources to browsers. This policy does not establish permission for that delivery. A short clip, remote URL or MIT data license does not change that conclusion. [Valve Video Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/video_policy)

The DotaTV policy covers community streams produced through the spectator tool. It requires original or licensed supporting content and separate authorization for an operator's camera work or broadcast audio. It provides no photo license for these trivia cards. [Dota 2 Community Stream Policy](https://www.dota2.com/dotatv)

The Steam Web API terms permit Steam Data in an identified application, subject to their conditions. They require a privacy policy, limited use of user data and clear separation from Valve endorsement. Their branding permission is tied to pages using Steam Data and the API documentation. They do not grant rights to arbitrary event photographs or all images hosted on a Steam CDN. [Steam Web API Terms of Use, sections 2, 3, 5 and 9](https://steamcommunity.com/dev/apiterms)

Valve's own site terms restrict reuse of materials from that site. The legal page identifies Valve marks and says other marks belong to their owners. Neither page supplies a general asset license. Team submission rules also require teams to own submitted material. Hosting a team logo therefore does not, by itself, establish that Valve owns it or permits this app to use it. [Valve site terms](https://www.valvesoftware.com/en/legal), [Valve legal information](https://store.steampowered.com/legal), [Dota team content rules](https://www.dota2.com/majorsregistration/content?l=english)

The inspected app has no payment, advertising, sponsorship, donation or paywall flow. Its privacy page and footers describe an unofficial project. This is evidence about this checkout, not a promise about a future business model. Keep that distinction if monetization is added.

The 23 unclear local photo/mark assets and all Flashback team-logo references were replaced with original graphics. The remaining game artwork and audio retain their existing use. Published terms and source records are documented, but this review does not turn them into a blanket redistribution grant. No one was contacted, and no permission was inferred from silence.
