# Design System: Queue Arcade LED board

This file describes the LED-board concept under `design/poc/led-board/` (lobby `index.html`, round `play.html`, `board.css`, `board.js`). It does not describe the shipped Next.js app, which keeps its own older look in CSS modules under `src/components`. Every value below is taken from the built concept.

The system has two layers. `board.css` is the shell: the clock over its time bar, cells that light by fill, one dot per percent in every split bar, one full-band colour flip at reveal, and reserved space for every phase. A skin is a second stylesheet, `theme-<game>.css`, loaded after the shell on a page whose body carries `skin-<game>`. It may change material, type faces, and ornament, and it may add that game's own ritual components inside the shell's modules. The one built skin is `theme-dota.css`, loaded by `index-dota.html` and `play-dota.html`; those pages add the skin's markup (the logo, the HUD bar, the ability-slot wells, the streak-name and gold-float slots) and `board.js` reads the body class for the grouped pick screen, the cooldown sweep, the day-night dial, and the HUD scores. Tokens prefixed `dota-` in the frontmatter belong to that skin; unprefixed tokens are the shell.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Arena Ribbon Board"**

The public room is one arena LED ribbon board. The page is a matte black floor ruled into 96px panels by faint seams. On the floor sit pure black LED modules: the clock, the question, four answer cells, and the scoreboard band. Everything that carries state is light. Words are condensed arena signage in capitals. Numbers are dot-matrix. Reading text is plain Barlow in sentence case.

Nothing fades, slides, or glows. A change of state is a one-frame swap, the way a scoreboard flips. The round has four phases (entry, answering, locked, reveal) and every element keeps its box through all four, so the player's eye never has to re-find the clock or the cells. At reveal the whole band drenches in the result colour for 700ms, then drops back to black with the result still lit.

The build rejects the dark dashboard of bordered cards with one glowing accent. It has no colour gradients, no glow halos, no shadows, no rounded corners, no transitions, and no borders used to show state.

The board is a shell, and a game pack dresses it with a skin. The Dota 2 skin keeps every mechanism above and dresses the board in the client's own rituals: slate instead of black, Dota gold for the live light, Radiant green and Dire red for the result, Radiance Bold for every piece of signage, and the client's own furniture inside the shell's modules. The clock module carries the HUD top bar (Radiant and Dire boxes around the clock, with the day-night dial over it). Each answer cell carries an ability-slot well with a hotkey badge and a cooldown sweep once the answer is locked. The state line becomes a kill-streak banner at reveal. Points carry a gold coin and a "+N" float. The pick screen groups heroes by attribute under the client's icons. The Fogged Vision portrait sits under fog of war. The lobby's Play now is Find Match green. A second game pack ships its own skin file and leaves the shell alone.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Matte ground, black modules, visible seams, zero radius.
- One colour per state: amber live, white locked or waiting, green right, red wrong, white-gold only for the 2x crowd-breaker.
- Unlit elements are drawn, not hidden: ghost dots at 16% opacity, unlit bar cells in ghost grey.
- Dot-matrix numerals (Doto), signage capitals (Barlow Condensed), reading text (Barlow).
- Every state change is instant. The only loops are the ticker scroll and the crowd-breaker blink.
- Every phase-dependent element reserves its space. Nothing moves between entry, answering, locked, and reveal.
- Two layers: the shell owns layout, geometry, bars, the reveal flip, and the state roles; a skin owns material, type faces, ornament, and the game's ritual components inside the shell's modules.

**The Shell-and-Skin Rule.** A skin is one stylesheet, `theme-<game>.css`, loaded after `board.css` on a `body.skin-<game>` page. It may change material, type faces, and ornament, and it may add game-ritual components inside the shell's modules (the HUD bar in the clock module, the ability-slot wells in the cells, the grouped pick screen in the hero picker). It may not change the module layout, the phase geometry, the one-dot-per-percent bars, the full-band reveal flip, or the roles in the State Colour Law. Every mechanism the shell documents is inherited by every skin unchanged.

## Colors

Five light colours on three blacks, with three warm greys for text that is drawn rather than lit.

### Primary
- **Amber** (`amber`, #ffb000): the live light. It lights the sign, the running clock, the lit time-bar cells, the "Answer now" state line, the keycaps, every inline numeral, the picked cell fill, the Play now action, the lobby headline's emphasised phrase, the focus ring, text selection, the input caret, and the scrollbar thumb. Amber means "live" and is never decoration.

### Secondary
- **Lit white** (`lit`, #f6f2e8): light that is on but not live. The question prompt, answer labels, the clock and state line while locked or waiting, the "Missed" result, the lobby headline, and the hover fill of the primary action.
- **Right green** (`green`, #3fe36f): the correct cell fill at reveal, the clock and state line on a right result, the correct hero tile's inset outline, and the correct split row.
- **Wrong red** (`red`, #ff3b3b): the fill of a cell the player picked and got wrong, the clock and state line on a wrong result, and the wrong hero tile's inset outline.

### Tertiary
- **Breaker gold** (`gold`, #ffe08a): reserved by law for the 2x crowd-breaker. It appears only on the keycap of the correct cell when the player was right and the room majority was wrong, blinking against module black.

### Neutral
- **Ground** (`ground`, #101010): the page floor under every module, ruled by seams.
- **Module** (`module`, #000000): every LED module, the fascia, and the ink on any lit surface (picked, correct, wrong, primary action, reveal flash).
- **Ghost** (`ghost`, #1b1b1b): an unlit pressable surface. Keycaps, the art module, the search input, hero tiles, and the hover state of cells, actions, and fascia controls.
- **Ghost 2** (`ghost-2`, #292929): unlit indicator cells. Time-bar cells, volume bars, fog-stage bars, the dark pixels of the sign mark, and the verdict placeholder dots.
- **Seam** (`seam`, 5% white): the ground's panel lines and the fascia control dividers.
- **Seam strong** (`seam-strong`, 14% white): the fascia bottom edge, the sign divider, scoreboard stat dividers, the Leave link divider, and the search input's resting stroke.
- **Text** (`text`, #ece7db): reading text (verdict, pitch, scoreboard note, Leave link, Steam label).
- **Text 2** (`text-2`, #a99f8b): secondary labels (round label, ticker, ticket, captions, cell sub-lines, unpicked cells after lock, muted controls).
- **Text 3** (`text-3`, #8f8676): the dimmest grey. Cells during entry, placeholder text, fixture slot names, grey LED separators, and the muted volume readout.

### Dota 2 skin
The skin re-points every shell colour variable on `:root` and adds the client's own accents. Each shell role keeps its job; only the light changes.

- **Dota gold** (`dota-live`): the amber role. Everything the shell lights amber (sign, running clock, lit time-bar cells, "Answer now", the ability-slot letters, inline numerals, picked cell fill, headline emphasis, focus ring, selection, caret, scrollbar) is Dota gold here, plus the ticker and round-label diamonds, the fog-stage bars, the sun in the day-night dial, the scoreboard numerals, the coin's gold float, and the current fixture's name.
- **Parchment** (`dota-lit`): the lit-white role. The prompt, answer labels, the clock at entry, lock, and reveal, the lobby headline, the streak name under the Streak stat, and the hover fill of a secondary action.
- **Radiant green** (`dota-green`): the right role. The correct cell fill, the clock on a right result, the correct tile's inset outline, the correct split row, the Radiant box's caption, its number at reveal, and its tint and border.
- **Dire red** (`dota-red`): the wrong role as a fill. The picked wrong cell fill, the clock on a wrong result, the wrong tile's inset outline, and the Dire box's tint and border.
- **Dire red text** (`dota-red-text`): the wrong role at small sizes, where the fill red is too dark to read on slate. The state line on a wrong result, the hero status on a wrong result, the wrong split row, and the Dire box's number at reveal.
- **Dire caption** (`dota-dire-caption`): the "answered wrong" caption inside the Dire box only.
- **Breaker gold** (`dota-gold`): the same white-gold as the shell, still reserved for the 2x crowd-breaker. It fills the correct cell's ability-slot well and blinks, and it draws the 2px inner frame on a picked cell's well.
- **Attribute colours** (`dota-str`, `dota-agi`, `dota-int`, `dota-all`): Strength red, Agility green, Intelligence blue, Universal violet. They mark a hero's primary attribute and nothing else: the 2px underline of a pick-screen group heading and the 8px square before a hero name in a split row. A hero with no attribute data gets the square in `dota-text-3`.
- **Find Match green** (`dota-play`, hover `dota-play-hover`, bottom edge `dota-play-edge`, ink `dota-play-ink`): the lobby's primary action only. It replaces the amber fill on Play now with the client's green, a 4px darker bottom edge, and white ink. White ink also sets the kill-streak banner text.
- **Fog** (`dota-fog`): the navy wash over the Fogged Vision portrait, at 78%, 50%, and 26% opacity across the three fog stages.
- **Slate ground** (`dota-ground`): the page floor, flat, with no seam grid. It is also the ink on every lit fill (picked, correct, wrong), the selection ink, and the dark slot inside an ability well once the cell is lit.
- **Well** (`dota-well`): the darkest surface, for anything that sits inside a module: the fascia, the art module, hero tiles, the search input, the dial, the ability-slot wells at rest, and the hotkey badges.
- **Inset** (`dota-inset`): the 1px dark line drawn inside every gold hairline frame.
- **Bevel** (`dota-bevel`): the 2px inner bevel of an ability-slot well at rest.
- **Module** (`dota-module`): every module, cell, and action at rest, and the sticky group heading's backing on the pick screen.
- **Ghost** (`dota-ghost`) and **Ghost 2** (`dota-ghost-2`): the same pressable-surface and unlit-indicator roles as the shell.
- **Gold seams** (`dota-seam` 14%, `dota-seam-strong` 34%, `dota-art-frame` 50%): three strengths of one gold. ### Named Rules
**The State Colour Law.** Amber is live answering. Lit white is locked, waiting, or missed. Green is right. Red is wrong. White-gold is the 2x crowd-breaker keycap and nothing else. A colour is never used as decoration.

**The Lit-or-Drawn Rule.** Every surface is either emitting light in one of the five colours or drawn unlit in a black or grey. There are no colour gradients, no glows, no translucent tints. A radial-gradient appears only as a dot stencil (the art module's screen-door overlay, the verdict placeholder field), never as a colour ramp.

**The Decisive Beat Rule.** The full band changes colour once per round, at reveal. The body takes flash-right, flash-wrong, or flash-missed for 700ms: every fascia, module, and cell background becomes the result colour (green, red, or lit white), all text and indicator cells become black, and images keep their pixels. The ground stays matte. The flash is then removed in one frame and the result colour stays only on the clock, the state line, and the result cells.

**The Attribute Mark Rule.** The four attribute colours are marks, not states. They appear only as the 2px underline of a pick-screen group heading and the 8px square before a split-row name. The name beside the square keeps the state colour. An attribute colour never fills a surface, never colours text, and never stands in for green or red.

**The Client Material Rule.** The Dota 2 skin carries four recorded exceptions to the Lit-or-Drawn Rule, each a material the client itself uses: the Radiant and Dire box tints (a vertical fade from 22% to 6% of the team colour), the kill-streak banner (deep red at the ends, brighter red at the centre), the gold coin (a radial from light gold to deep gold), and the fog-of-war wash (a translucent navy over the portrait). No other surface in the skin carries a gradient or a tint, and none of these four is ever used as a state.

**The Small Red Rule.** The fill red (`dota-red`) is a surface colour. Red text smaller than the clock uses `dota-red-text`, and the Dire caption uses `dota-dire-caption`, so a wrong result stays readable on slate.

## Elevation & Depth

There are no shadows. Depth comes from three blacks: the matte ground (#101010) with its seam lines, the deeper module black (#000000) sitting on it, and the ghost fill (#1b1b1b) that marks a pressable or unlit surface inside a module. Hover is a swap from module black to ghost. Press and focus are the amber outline.

Two dot stencils add texture without light: the art module's screen-door overlay (40% black dots on a 3px grid over the artwork) and the verdict placeholder field (ghost-2 dots on an 8px grid). Fogged Vision hides the hero behind a filter that steps from blur 18px, saturate 0.35, contrast 0.6, brightness 0.7 at scale 1.12 (stage 3) through blur 9px (stage 2) and blur 4px (stage 1) to clear (stage 0). The stages change at 10 and 5 seconds left, with no transition.

In the Dota 2 skin the ground is flat slate with no seam grid. The ladder is four steps in blue-black: the well (the darkest, for anything set into a module: fascia, art, tiles, search, dial, ability wells, hotkey badges), the ground, the module, and the ghost. Every module, cell, and action carries a frame: a 1px gold hairline (seam-strong) over a 1px inset line in the dark inset colour, drawn with a zero-blur inset box-shadow. The frame is material, not elevation: every panel at rest has the same frame, so no panel sits higher than another. A lit fill (picked, correct, wrong, the Play action) drops the inset line, so state is still shown by fill alone. An ability-slot well is the one bevelled surface: a 1px seam-strong border over a 2px inner bevel, which becomes a 2px breaker-gold inner frame when the cell is picked and disappears when the cell lights at reveal. The art module's screen-door stencil and the verdict's dot-field stencil are removed; the art gets a 2px gold frame with a 1px black outline inset 3px, and the verdict becomes a plain plate with a 1px seam top rule.

Fogged Vision becomes fog of war. A navy wash (`dota-fog`) lies over the portrait at 78% (stage 3), 50% (stage 2), and 26% (stage 1), and the image under it steps from blur 12px, saturate 0.4, scale 1.1 through blur 6px, saturate 0.6, scale 1.06 and blur 2px, saturate 0.8, scale 1.03 to clear. The fog-stage bars sit above the wash. The stages still change at 10 and 5 seconds left with no transition.

### Named Rules
**The Three Blacks Rule.** Ground, module, and ghost are the only depth. A surface never gets a shadow, a glow, a border, or a tint to lift it.

**The Hairline Frame Rule.** In the Dota 2 skin a frame is a 1px gold hairline over a 1px dark inset line, with no blur and no offset, drawn on every module, cell, and action at rest. The frame is never a state: a surface that lights by fill loses its inset line for as long as it is lit.

## Shapes

Every shape is a rectangle with zero radius. Modules, cells, keycaps, actions, inputs, hero tiles, and LED squares are all square-cornered. Modules have no border; they are separated by 12px of ground. The only strokes are 1px seams: the fascia edges and dividers, scoreboard stat dividers, fixture row edges, and the search input's resting stroke.

State is shown by fill, not by border. A picked cell fills amber, a correct cell fills green, a wrong cell fills red, and the keycap inverts to black on the cell's colour. The one exception is image tiles in the hero grid, where a fill would hide the art: hover and focus draw a 3px amber outline inset, picked a 4px amber outline inset, and reveal a 4px green or red outline inset while every other tile drops to 25% opacity.

Small LED squares are the system's only mark. A 6px amber or grey square separates label items in the ticker, round label, scoring line, and status line. A 5px grey square separates ticket items. The sign carries a 3x3 grid of 5px pixels with alternating amber and ghost-2. Doto's dots, the time bar's 15 cells, the volume slider's ten 8x18 bars, the fog-stage's three 44x6 bars, and the 100-dot split bars are the same vocabulary.

The Dota 2 skin keeps zero radius on every panel and adds two shapes of its own that are round because the client's are: the day-night dial, a 76x38 half-disc (56x28 under 720px) rimmed in seam-strong with a 2px seam-strong horizon along its flat edge, and the 18px gold coin before the points stat. Nothing else is rounded. The LED separator becomes a 6px gold diamond (a square rotated 45 degrees) in the ticker and round label; in the ticket, scoring line, and hero status it is a text-3 diamond that turns gold when the hero pick is locked. The sign's 3x3 pixel mark is hidden and the Dota 2 logo, 30px square (24px under 720px), stands before the sign. Each answer cell carries a 56px ability-slot well (40px under 720px) with an 18x16 hotkey badge (14x13) overlapping its bottom-right corner by 1px, a padlock drawn as a 22px square-cornered outline, and a cooldown sweep drawn by a conic gradient. The pick screen's group headings carry a 2px attribute-coloured underline, and split rows carry an 8px attribute square before the hero name. The art module trades its dot stencil for a 2px gold frame with a 1px black outline inset 3px. The Radiant and Dire boxes and the streak banner carry 1px borders as part of their material (the team colour at 45%, white at 18%), and Play now carries a 4px darker bottom edge; none of these borders is a state. The gold hairline on every panel (see Elevation & Depth) is the skin's stroke, and the fascia, scoreboard, and fixture seams turn gold too.

### Motion
**The Instant Swap Rule.** No element has a transition. Every state change (phase, pick, lock, reveal, hover, fog stage) is a one-frame swap of class and colour. The only animations are the ticker scroll (48s linear loop, paused on hover), the crowd-breaker keycap blink (0.5s, steps(1)), and the 700ms reveal flash, which is a class added and removed by script. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the ticker stops scrolling and steps in place, the keycap blink is removed, and the flash remains as a single instant swap.

The Dota 2 skin adds no transitions. It re-declares the crowd-breaker blink on the ability well with its own colours (breaker gold to slate, letter slate to gold, 0.5s, steps(1)). Two values are driven by script in 200ms steps with no easing: the dial's turn across the round, and the cooldown sweep's remaining fraction on the picked well. The reveal flash lights every framed surface in the result colour as before.

### Browser surfaces

In the Dota 2 skin the same surfaces take Dota gold, and selection ink is slate.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** set every readout (clock, percent, points, counts) in Doto with tabular numerals.
- **Do** reserve the box for every phase-dependent element: minimum heights on text, fixed heights on footers and split rows, visibility: hidden for content that is not yet shown.
- **Do** show state by fill: a picked cell fills amber, a right cell fills green, a wrong cell fills red, each with black ink and an inverted keycap.
- **Do** draw unlit dots and cells rather than hiding them: 16% opacity for split dots, ghost-2 for bar cells.
- **Do** separate modules by 12px of ground and join stats or rows with 1px seams.
- **Do** keep the 2px amber focus ring with 2px offset on every control.
- **Do** use a 6px LED square between label items in place of punctuation.
- **Do** swap colour across the whole band once at reveal, for 700ms, and leave the ground matte.
- **Do** ship a new game as one `theme-<game>.css` loaded after `board.css` on a `body.skin-<game>` page, changing material, type faces, and ornament, and adding that game's rituals only inside the shell's modules.
- **Do** reserve the box for every skin component before it has content: an en dash in the HUD numbers, a transparent border and padding on the state line, 4.5ch for the gold float, 1.3em for the streak name.
- **Do** keep the inset line off every lit fill in the Dota 2 skin: picked, correct, wrong, and Play now show no dark inset.
- **Do** keep attribute colours to the 2px group-heading underline and the 8px split-row square.
- **Do** set every Dota 2 signage role and readout in Radiance 700 and leave reading text in Barlow.
- **Do** use Dire red text, not the fill red, for red text smaller than the clock in the Dota 2 skin.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add transitions, easing, fades, or slides. Every change is instant.
- **Don't** use colour gradients, glows, blur halos, tints, or shadows. A radial gradient is only ever a dot stencil. The Dota 2 skin's four client materials (HUD tints, streak banner, coin, fog wash) are the recorded exceptions and are never states.
- **Don't** round a corner. The Dota 2 skin's half-disc dial and round coin are the recorded exceptions.
- **Don't** use amber for anything that is not live. It is a state, not an accent.
- **Don't** use breaker gold anywhere but the 2x crowd-breaker keycap or well, and the picked well's inner frame in the Dota 2 skin.
- **Don't** show state with a border. Fill the surface. Inset outlines are allowed only on image tiles where a fill would hide the art.
- **Don't** let a phase change move, resize, or reflow anything on the board.
- **Don't** set a heading, label, or button in sentence case or in Barlow. Signage is Barlow Condensed uppercase; in the Dota 2 skin it is Radiance 700 uppercase.
- **Don't** let a skin change the module layout, the phase geometry, the 100-dot bars, the reveal flip, or which role a state colour plays.
- **Don't** use an attribute colour for state, text, or a fill.
- **Don't** use Find Match green anywhere but the lobby's primary action.
- **Don't** add a second display face to the Dota 2 skin. Radiance is the one signage face, and no serif stands in for it.
